Who we are

About the Sangguniang Bayan

The municipal legislative body serving the people and communities of Irosin.

Legislating for the public good

The Sangguniang Bayan ng Irosin is the legislative body of the Municipality of Irosin, Sorsogon. It is composed of elected municipal councilors who craft ordinances, adopt resolutions, and oversee local policies that promote the welfare, development, and good governance of the municipality.

This official page provides the public with access to information about the Sangguniang Bayan, its members, barangay assignments, standing committees, and approved legislative measures.

Rooted in community.
Focused on progress.

Through deliberation, representation, and responsive local legislation, the Sangguniang Bayan works toward a resilient, inclusive, and progressive Irosin.

Service · Accountability · Good governance
Municipal direction

Vision, Mission & Quality Policy

Guiding commitments of the Municipality of Irosin.

01 / VISION

Our Vision

A livable, resilient, ecologically balanced and progressive center of agro-industry, eco-tourism, commerce and trade in the province, with people who are God-loving, healthy, globally competitive, gender sensitive and participative, nurtured by a just, transparent and accountable governance.

02 / MISSION

Our Mission

To promote the general welfare of Irosanons and develop all human resources through comprehensive and integrated delivery of basic services, enhancement of multi-sectoral participation and mustering of resources towards sustainable development.

03 / QUALITY POLICY

Our Commitment

We commit to improve the quality of life and ensure sustainable development; deliver basic services and infrastructure; address stakeholder needs; satisfy applicable requirements; achieve our goals; and continually improve our processes and Quality Management System.
